## General Design Guidelines

### Brand Consistency

- **Primary Color:** #8AC249 (vibrant green) - use throughout
- **Stock Warnings:** Orange (#FF9800) for low stock, Red for out of stock
- **Dark Mode:** #222222 background with same green accents
- **Fonts:** Use brand assets (Inter for English, NotoKufiArabic for Arabic)

### Device Frames

- Use modern, clean device frames
- iOS: iPhone 14 Pro Max size (6.7")
- Android: Equivalent flagship size
- Consider subtle shadows/depth for premium feel

### Visual Hierarchy

- Screenshots 1-3: Priority features (Hero, Home, NEW feature)
- Screenshots 4-7: Core functionality
- Screenshot 8: Optional (use if you need 8 total)

### Arabic-Specific Considerations

- **RTL Layout:** Everything mirrors (not just text)
- **Text Length:** Arabic often 20-30% longer - plan spacing
- **Typography:** Proper Arabic fonts essential (NotoKufiArabic)
- **Numbers:** Use Arabic numerals (٠-٩) OR Western (0-9) - be consistent
- **Time Format:** "٢:٠٠ مساءً" (2:00 PM) or "١٤:٠٠" (14:00) - your choice

### What Makes Great App Store Screenshots

- **First 3 screens = most important** (appear without scrolling)
- **Show actual UI** (not just marketing graphics)
- **Highlight unique features** (5 follow-ups, refill customization)
- **Use real-looking data** (relatable medication names)
- **Color-coding matters** (green/orange/red stock levels)
- **Clean, uncluttered** (every element has purpose)
