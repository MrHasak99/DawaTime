## 3. Design Language Guidelines

### Visual Hierarchy

**Priority order in any screenshot:**

1. **Primary Action** (e.g., "Save Medication" button, "+")
2. **Key Information** (medication name, time, stock level)
3. **Supporting Details** (type, frequency, dates)
4. **Background Elements** (cards, containers, navigation)

**Application:**

- Use size, color, and whitespace to guide eye
- Green (#8AC249) = primary actions and positive states
- Orange/Red = warnings and negative states
- Gray = neutral/inactive elements

---

### Typography Hierarchy

**Text Sizes (relative guidelines):**

1. **Headlines:** Large, bold (Nunito 800) - "Never Miss a Dose"
2. **Titles:** Medium, semi-bold (Inter 600 or NotoKufiArabic 700) - "My Medications"
3. **Body:** Regular (Inter 400 or NotoKufiArabic 400) - medication names, descriptions
4. **Labels:** Small, regular or medium - field labels, timestamps

**Application:**

- Maintain 2-3 size jumps maximum between levels
- Arabic text needs slightly larger sizes (more vertical space)
- Ensure readability at thumbnail size (App Store preview)

---

### Color Psychology

**Brand Green (#8AC249):**

- **Meaning:** Health, growth, vitality, trust
- **Use:** Primary actions, healthy stock, brand elements
- **Avoid:** Overuse (becomes overwhelming)

**Warning Orange (#FF9800):**

- **Meaning:** Caution, attention needed, but not critical
- **Use:** Low stock warnings, important notifications
- **Pair with:** Icons (⚠️) to reinforce meaning

**Alert Red:**

- **Meaning:** Urgent, action required immediately
- **Use:** Out of stock, critical alerts only
- **Pair with:** Strong language ("Refill needed!")

**Dark Mode Gray (#222222):**

- **Meaning:** Modern, sophisticated, reduces eye strain
- **Use:** Dark theme background
- **Pair with:** Same green accent (don't change brand color)

---

### Whitespace Strategy

**Purpose:** Prevent overwhelming users with information density.

**Guidelines:**

- **Breathing room:** 16-24px between major sections
- **Card padding:** 16-20px internal padding minimum
- **Button padding:** 12-16px vertical, 24-32px horizontal
- **Line height:** 1.5x for body text, 1.2x for headlines

**Application:**

- More whitespace = premium feel
- Less whitespace = information-dense (avoid)
- Balance efficiency with clarity

## 4. Arabic Design Considerations

### RTL Layout Principles

**Not Just Mirroring:**

- Navigation flow: Right → Left (back button on right, forward on left)
- Reading order: Top-right → bottom-left
- Visual weight: Right side feels "heavier" in RTL
- Icons: Some flip (arrows), some don't (clocks, pills)

**What to Mirror:**

- Navigation arrows (< becomes >)
- Swipe gestures direction
- Card alignment (title on right, icon on left)
- Form labels (right-aligned)

**What NOT to Mirror:**

- Brand logo (DawaTime stays same direction)
- Icons (pills, clocks, settings gear)
- Numbers (even in Arabic)
- Visual metaphors (up/down, progression)

---

### Arabic Typography Best Practices

**Font:** NotoKufiArabic (from brand assets)

- **Weight 400:** Body text, labels
- **Weight 700:** Headings, emphasis

**Spacing:**

- Arabic letters connect → less letterspacing needed
- Word spacing more generous than English
- Line height +20-30% compared to English (Arabic is taller)

**Text Length:**

- Arabic often 20-30% longer than English equivalent
- Plan UI elements with expansion in mind
- Test with actual text, not Lorem Ipsum

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

- ❌ Using English fonts for Arabic text (garbled)
- ❌ Justifying Arabic text (creates awkward gaps)
- ❌ Ignoring diacritics/tashkeel (makes text taller)
- ❌ Mixing Arabic and English in same line unnecessarily

---

### Cultural Appropriateness (Kuwait/GCC)

**Healthcare Context:**

- Medication management is sensitive topic
- Tone should be: Helpful, not patronizing
- Privacy matters (don't show real names/data)
- Religious consideration: Friday/Saturday = weekend (not Saturday/Sunday)

**Color Associations:**

- Green = Islam, nature, health ✅
- Red = caution (not anger) ✅
- Blue = trust (but DawaTime is green-branded)
- Yellow = attention (use sparingly)

**Language Tone:**

- Formal Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic)
- Respectful addressing (أنت not إنت)
- Avoid slang or dialect-specific phrases
- Medical terms: Use standard Arabic medical terminology

## 6. Technical Specifications

### Device Frames

**iOS:**

- iPhone 14 Pro Max (6.7" display)
- OR iPhone 15 Pro Max if you prefer latest
- Avoid showing notch/Dynamic Island prominently
- Use clean, modern frame style

**Android:**

- Samsung Galaxy S23/S24 equivalent size
- OR Google Pixel 8 Pro
- Avoid manufacturer-specific UI elements
- Use clean, stock Android appearance

---

### File Naming Convention

**Suggested format:**

```
dawatime_ios_01_hero_en.png
dawatime_ios_02_home_en.png
dawatime_ios_03_refill_en.png
...
dawatime_ios_01_hero_ar.png
dawatime_ios_02_home_ar.png
...
dawatime_android_01_hero_en.png
...
```

**Benefits:**

- Easy to organize
- Platform clear (ios/android)
- Order obvious (01-08)
- Screen purpose clear (hero, home, refill)
- Language clear (en/ar)

---

### Resolution Requirements

**iOS App Store:**

- 1290 × 2796 pixels (6.7" display)
- PNG format
- RGB color space
- 72 DPI minimum (higher is fine)

**Google Play:**

- 1080 × 2400 pixels (common flagship size)
- PNG format
- RGB color space
- Max 8 MB per image

**General:**

- Export at 2x or 3x resolution for sharpness
- Compress without quality loss (TinyPNG, etc.)
- Test at thumbnail size (must be readable)

## 9. Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Content Mistakes

- ❌ Using stock photos of people (generic, impersonal)
- ❌ Showing real user data (privacy issue)
- ❌ Mixing languages in single screenshot (confusing)
- ❌ Too much text (users won't read)
- ❌ Vague benefit statements ("Easy to use" - what does that mean?)

### Design Mistakes

- ❌ Inconsistent device frames (some iOS, some Android in same set)
- ❌ Poor Arabic typography (wrong fonts, bad spacing)
- ❌ Color mismatches (#8AC249 vs #7FB23A)
- ❌ Cluttered layouts (every pixel filled)
- ❌ Unreadable at thumbnail size

### Technical Mistakes

- ❌ Wrong resolution (blurry when uploaded)
- ❌ JPEG format (artifacts around text)
- ❌ sRGB color space (colors look wrong on some devices)
- ❌ Files too large (>8 MB, won't upload to Play Store)
- ❌ Missing platform (only did iOS, forgot Android)

### Strategic Mistakes

- ❌ Hiding unique features (5 follow-ups not shown)
- ❌ Showing generic features only (everyone has reminders)
- ❌ No clear value proposition (why DawaTime vs competitors?)
- ❌ Forgetting target market (Kuwait/GCC, Arabic-first)
